01 — Featured Project

Design & Technology Research Lead

Huddle Health.

An inner-ear wearable that combines impact force tracking and real-time brain activity monitoring into a single device — giving parents, coaches, and trainers the data they need to protect young athletes on the field.

The Problem

Youth sports are in the middle of a brain health crisis. Up to 50% of concussions go unreported, participation in tackle football has dropped 29% since 2016, and current technology only measures either impact force or brain activity — never both at once. There is no single, all-in-one wearable that gives a complete picture of what's happening inside a young athlete's head during play.

The Solution

Huddle Health is an inner-ear wearable that tracks both impact force and brain activity during play. By leveraging the inner ear — considered the gold standard for measuring brain movement — the device delivers clinical-grade accuracy in a form factor built for the field. Real-time alerts notify coaches and parents the moment a dangerous hit occurs, replacing guesswork with data.

⚡

Real-Time Impact Detection

Accelerometers and gyroscopes capture every hit, classifying severity and sending instant sideline alerts.

🧠

EEG Brain Monitoring

In-ear EEG sensors track neural activity continuously, detecting concussion-level events that external sensors miss.

📊

Analytics Dashboard

A comprehensive platform for coaches, trainers, and parents to track impact history, brain strain, and season-long player readiness.

Why Current Solutions Fall Short

No existing product combines impact tracking and brain monitoring in a single real-time device. Huddle Health is the first to do both.
